Leaflet 1.9.4 is distributed under its BSD license in `leaflet/LICENSE`.
The map uses the published Leaflet JS distribution and its upstream CSS.
Mapbox vector-tile, pbf and point-geometry source and their licenses are in
`vector/`. Only the module import for point-geometry has been rewritten to
resolve locally. Public vector tiles are served by OpenFreeMap with OpenMapTiles
and OpenStreetMap attribution on the map. The station data has separate CC0
provenance under `assets/stations/`. Third-party sources are excluded from
authored-code formatting.
